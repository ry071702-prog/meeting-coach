# Meeting plan template

Write in the user's language. Fits on one screen. Everything about the other person or their company has a source.

```markdown
## <date> <role of the other person> — <meeting kind> plan

### Goal
<one line: what the user wants to leave with>

### About them                     (3–6 lines, each with [n] pointing to Sources)
- <fact> [1]

### Career timeline               (from sources; most recent first; note conflicts between sources)
| When | Role / event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM | <role, project or talk> | [n] |

### Reading them                   (the analysis: what they value and how to talk with them)
- **What they value** — 2–3 points, each backed by their own words in quotes with year and source: 「<their words>」 (YYYY) [n]
- **What they care about now** — their latest public topic or project and how it touches the user's topic [n]
- **How they like to talk and decide** — inferred from interviews, talks or earlier notes; label as 推測 / hypothesis
- **Common ground with the user** — shared background or interests found in the sources or earlier notes
- **What they may want from this meeting** — hypothesis
- **What will land / what to avoid** — 2–3 each, tied to the points above (e.g. bring a decision you made yourself, not a request to be told what to do)

### Since last time                (only if earlier notes were given)
- Agreed: <what was decided>
- They said they would: <...>   / You said you would: <...> (done? say so first)
- Already answered — do not ask again: <...>

### About their company            (external meetings, or 2–3 lines for another company in the same group; facts with dates, then hypotheses labelled as such)
- <fact, YYYY-MM> [2]
- 推測 / Hypothesis: <what they may care about> → check with question <k>

### Keep in mind this time         (1–3, from your own history)
- <concrete reminder> (A-YYYYMMDD-n)

### Opening line
「<one sentence: purpose of the meeting and what you hope to leave with>」

### Questions
| # | Phase | Question | Aim | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | open | 「…」 | <generic aim> | [1] |

### Avoid
- <one or two phrasings to avoid, tied to your habits>

### Before you close, confirm
- <who does what by when>

### Sources
1. <title> — <URL> (<date if any>)

### Not found / left out
- <e.g. no public interview found / a same-name person excluded>
```

After showing the plan, save it with `learnings.py prep-save` (see SKILL.md). Only the goal, kind, length, focus ids and the question aims are stored — not the facts, sources or wording.
