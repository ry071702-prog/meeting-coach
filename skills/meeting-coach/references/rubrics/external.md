# Type: external (sales call, client meeting, partner meeting, first appointment)

Rubric key: `external`. Weights: `config.default.json → rubrics.external`. Level anchors: `criteria.md`.

## What a good one looks like
The user learns the other side's situation before pitching, checks that the proposal landed, and leaves with a dated next step agreed by both sides.

## Reading the criteria for this type
- **discovery** replaces generic questioning: did the user learn the problem, who decides, budget or constraints if relevant, and timing?
- **next_meeting_secured** is added. A polite close without a date is the most common gap.
- **participation_balance**: in a discovery-stage meeting (first or early meetings, needs not yet known) the other side should talk at least about as much as the user. In a proposal-stage meeting the user may talk more, but every block of explanation should get a substantive reply. A long pitch answered only with backchannels is a finding at either stage. If the stage is unclear, ask the user.
- **reaction_handling** carries most of the "polite no" detection. In many cultures, and very often in Japanese business settings, a refusal sounds like 「前向きに検討します」「一度持ち帰らせてください」「社内で相談します」. Treat these as **unresolved** unless the same stretch contains an owner, a date, or a concrete condition.
